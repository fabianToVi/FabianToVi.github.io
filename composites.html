Manufacturing of Polymer Composites

2026

Lab coursework in composite materials manufacturing and structural testing, covering wet lamination with vacuum bagging, prepreg layup, vacuum-assisted resin infusion (VARI), and thermoplastic composite processing. Worked with multiple reinforcement fibers (carbon, glass, and flax), each with distinct handling and consolidation requirements, and also covered sandwich composite construction, comparing core material options (e.g. foam, honeycomb, balsa) and their appropriate use cases.
