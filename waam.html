Fatigue Characterization of Wire-Arc Additively Manufactured (WAAM) Patches with Various Geometries

September 2026 – Ongoing

Ongoing semester project at Empa (the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology), done as part of my Master's studies. WAAM can be used to print patches onto cracked steel structures and extend their fatigue life, but the patch itself can become the starting point for new cracks. I use finite element analysis following IIW guidelines to understand how patch geometry shapes the stresses at the patch edge, then develop the printing parameters for different print strategies and fatigue test the resulting patches. The aim is to make repairing steel structures a viable alternative to replacing them, saving material and cost.
