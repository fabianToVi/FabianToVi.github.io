Design for Additive Manufacturing

2025

Together with five teammates, I worked on "Stator Tooth Solutions for Axial Flux Machines," a group project through ETH Zurich's Product Development Group (PDZ), exploring how additive manufacturing can improve cooling and reduce eddy current losses in axial flux motor stators. As a team, we designed and 3D printed ten different stator tooth geometries, simulated eddy current losses in COMSOL, and tested their performance experimentally via liquid cooling. I designed two of the tooth geometries: a serpentine tooth, a folded wall that forms internal channels so coolant runs in direct contact with the tooth's core, and a Hilbert-curve tooth, a fractal pattern that breaks up the material into a fine, meandering path to interrupt eddy current loops while maximizing cooling surface. I also took part in the L-PBF printing and post-processing of the parts, and helped run the experimental cooling tests, including plotting and analyzing the thermal data.
